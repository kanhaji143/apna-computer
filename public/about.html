Our Story

One platform, nine solutions

Apna Computer began with a simple idea — local schools, shops and coaching centres should get the same technology big brands use, but in a simple and affordable way.

So we built different business modules on one platform — school, shop, marketplace, coaching, security agency and real estate. Each module feels like a top website in its sector, and comes with a powerful admin panel too.
