About Us

Technology tutorials written in plain, simple English.

Apna Computer Blog is a free learning resource run by Apna Computer Ltd. We publish practical, step-by-step tutorials that help students, job seekers and complete beginners get comfortable with technology — without confusing jargon.

What we publish

  • AI Tools — practical guides to ChatGPT, Gemini, image generators and other AI tools you can use for study and work.
  • Computer Basics — from turning on a computer to file management, internet safety and everyday software.
  • Excel & Power BI — spreadsheets, formulas, dashboards and data skills that employers actually ask for.
  • Digital Skills — typing, online forms, government portals, email and the everyday tools of digital India.

How we write

Every article is written to be followed along step by step. We use short sentences, real examples and screenshots where they help. If a tutorial needs a tool, we tell you what it costs (or that it is free) before you start.

We do not publish copied content, and we do not make claims we cannot back up. If we make a mistake, we correct it and note the update date on the article.
