How confirmation works

When a visitor subscribes, their email is stored as Pending with a consent timestamp. Connecting an email service (Brevo / Mailchimp / SendGrid) sends a confirmation link — once clicked, the status becomes Confirmed and tutorials can be sent. Every marketing email must include an unsubscribe link. Emails are never shared or sold.
