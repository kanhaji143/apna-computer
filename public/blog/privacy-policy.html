Information collected automatically

We never sell, rent or trade your personal information to third parties.

When you subscribe we store your email as "pending" and only start sending tutorials once you confirm via the confirmation email. The consent checkbox is never pre-ticked. Every email we send contains a one-click unsubscribe link, and you can also contact us directly to be removed. Consent records are kept so we can prove opt-in.
